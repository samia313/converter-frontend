# ❓ COMMON QUESTIONS

## Q: How long until all pages are indexed?
**A:** 2-4 weeks for all 2,374 pages to be indexed by Google. Bing usually faster (1-2 weeks).

## Q: Can I speed up indexing?
**A:** 
1. Use "Request Indexing" tool in Google Search Console
2. Get backlinks from other websites
3. Share content on social media
4. Update pages regularly

## Q: Why aren't all pages indexed yet?
**A:** Normal! Google indexes incrementally:
- Week 1: 30-40% of pages
- Week 2: 60-70% of pages
- Week 3: 85-95% of pages
- Week 4: 95-99% of pages

## Q: Do I need to resubmit the sitemap?
**A:** NO! Once submitted, Google & Bing automatically:
- Crawl every 1-2 weeks
- Discover new pages automatically
- Update existing pages
- No manual resubmission needed

## Q: What if pages aren't indexing?
**A:** Check:
1. robots.txt isn't blocking crawlers
2. pages have unique titles/descriptions
3. no noindex meta tags
4. pages load correctly
5. no redirect loops
